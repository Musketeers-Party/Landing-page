How it works

A mobile-first experience: find "Gems" on a map, walk toward a coordinate, and unlock anonymous messages within a 15–20 m radius. Position is a core part of the unlock logic, so permissions are handled carefully and the data stays anonymous.

  • View Gems on a map
  • Move to a coordinate
  • Unlock a message within 15–20 m
